## Verma et al. — CAS-DMET natural orbitals and complete degeneracy

Shreya Verma, Abhishek Mitra, Yu Jin, Soumi Haldar, Christian Vorwerk, Matthew R. Hermes, Giulia Galli, Laura Gagliardi,
"Optical Properties of Neutral F Centers in Bulk MgO with Density Matrix Embedding," Journal of Physical Chemistry Letters 14(34), 7703–7710 (2023).
DOI: 10.1021/acs.jpclett.3c01875
Primary URL: https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jpclett.3c01875
Public record: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37606586/
Preprint: https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv-2023-z0vxs

The Supporting Information explicitly compares RHF- and ROHF-derived CAS-DMET natural-orbital baths. It reports a case in which an active space contains only one orbital from a triply degenerate t2g set; the authors state that absence of the complete t2g set breaks the degeneracy and is therefore incorrect. Their ROHF bath recovers the symmetrically correct natural-orbital set.

Relevance to OES:
- direct prior art for the physical requirement that an active/embedding-space truncation must not retain only part of a degenerate multiplet when doing so breaks the system symmetry;
- strongly constrains novelty claims around the OES rule that complete degenerate orbital blocks are indivisible;
- closely parallels the OES He/Li observation that arbitrary orbital-by-orbital truncation breaks a p-like manifold while complete blocks restore it;
- OES may still differ in using complete-degenerate-block admission as a hard fixed-width allocation/validation rule across its model-chemistry ladder, but the underlying symmetry principle is established.
